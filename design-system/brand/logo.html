Verifi Design System/Brand/Logo

Logo.

Three words that are not interchangeable, one colour, and a margin nothing may enter.

Symbol, wordmark, logotype

WordWhat it means
SymbolThe two angled strokes on their own. It may stand alone only in tightly controlled places — avatars, favicons — or where the full name appears close by.
WordmarkThe word “verifi” on its own, without the strokes.
LogotypeBoth together. This is the normal logo and what most people mean.
The symbol is not a shortcut. It must not be separated from the Verifi name unless a special case has been approved.

Clear space and minimum size

Leave a margin equal to the height of the symbol on all four sides. Nothing goes in it: no text, no rule, no part of a photograph, no other company’s logo.

WhereSmallestWhy
On screen96 × 24 pxBelow this the counters in the e and the fi fill in.
In print25 mm wideInk spread closes the same gaps.
Stitched, etched, cast40 mm wideThe two strokes of the symbol merge into one.

What colour it is

On lighter backgrounds
#2E2B27 · Pantone 426
On darker backgrounds
#FFFFFF

Black or white only. There is no blue version, no two-tone version, no gradient.

One exception. Verifi Yellow may be used on imagery, in special and considered cases, where the contrast is high. That is a Design decision, not a per-project one.

Placement and sizing

  • In marketing layoutsThe logotype sits at the bottom. Where possible the centre of the symbol aligns to the centre of the artboard.
  • With the fifth elementIt aligns to the centre of the “road” the banding creates.
  • In the productTop-left, in the sidebar.
  • In documentsTop-left or bottom-right. Not both.
  • Against a headlineIts height is 3/3, 2/3 or 1/3 of the headline height.
  • MaximumNever more than 50% of the artboard width.
