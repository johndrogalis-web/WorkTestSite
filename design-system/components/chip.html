Chip

A pressable pill with a status dot: Information, Offline, Live or Maintenance, in two sizes, with an optional icon and dismiss.

Has gaps4 modes4 states × 2 sizesLight and dark readFigma 62644:15248 ↗Light 64777:75691 ↗Dark 64777:75753 ↗
What changed in this version (October 2026). Rebuilt against Trinity Design System John V. The chip went from one neutral style in two sizes to four modes (Information, Offline, Live, Maintenance), each with Default / Hover / Focus / Active, with an 8px indicator dot, a trailing icon slot and a dismiss button, and real dark-mode tokens. Every colour is a chip/<mode>/* token. The neutral chip on the old page maps to Offline.
What it is. The thing you press to filter, select or jump, that also tells you whether the thing it names is alive.

The four modes, left to right: Information, Offline, Live, Maintenance. Each chip is an indicator dot, a label, an optional trailing icon and an optional dismiss. Large is 32px, Small 24px. Dark is read from the file, not derived.

Try it

Hover, Tab to, and click a chip. Click selects it (the Active fill); click again clears. The × removes it. Each chip is one <button aria-pressed>; the × is a second button inside the same pill.

Anatomy

  • ContainerPill, radius 100, 1px container padding carrying the outer stroke, then a 1px inner stroke (2px on focus). Large 32px max-height, padding 6, gap 5; Small 24px.
  • Indicator dot8px, mode colour, #FFFFFF stroke. The dot is the mode; it is never hidden when the chip carries status.
  • LabelBody/sm 14, mode colour. One or two words: a place, a unit, a filter value.
  • Trailing icon (optional)12px Icon XSmall. An arrow when the chip navigates; otherwise nothing.
  • Dismiss (optional)Icon-only small: 24px element, 18px background, 12px ×. Present when the chip is a removable filter.
Chip, Tag, Badge. A Chip is interactive and carries a mode. A Tag is a static blue label. A Badge is a count or a status word on something else. If it does nothing when clicked, it is not a chip.

Behaviour

These are rules, not suggestions. Figma draws 4 modes × 4 states × 2 sizes in light and dark. What a chip does when pressed, removed or grouped is the house rule. Build to this.
  • A chip is a button. Press selects it (Active), press again clears. In a single-select set, selecting one clears the others; in multi-select they accumulate.
  • Or a chip is a link. With a trailing arrow and an href, pressing navigates and there is no Active state. Never both.
  • Dismiss removes, it does not deselect. The × takes the chip out of the set and fires dismissed. Focus moves to the next chip, or the previous if it was last, or to the set’s container.
  • Mode is status, not decoration. Live means a heartbeat in the last interval; Offline means none; Maintenance means someone set it. Information is everything else. The mode changes when the data does, not on click.
  • Sets wrap. A row of chips wraps to the next line at 5px gaps; it never scrolls sideways and never truncates to “+3” below eight chips.
  • Keyboard. Tab into the set, arrows between chips, Space / Enter to press, Delete or Backspace to dismiss a dismissable chip.
  • Label length. Two words. A chip that needs three is a Tag in a list, not a chip.

Guidelines

Do

  • Use the mode the data has: Live, Offline, Maintenance only when they are true.
  • Use Small inside tables and card headers.
  • Show the × only when the person is allowed to remove the chip.
  • Keep one size and one mode family per set.
  • Name the set: “Filters”, “Plants”.

Do not

  • Use Live or Maintenance as a colour choice.
  • Put a chip where a Badge belongs (a count on a tab).
  • Make the dot the only difference between two chips with the same label.
  • Use a chip for a destructive action.
  • Disable a chip; remove it.

Accessibility

  • Two targets, two buttons. The pill is a <button aria-pressed>; the × is its own <button> named “Remove <label>”. Never one button with two jobs.
  • Mode in words. The dot is decorative; the status is in the accessible name (“Truck 45730, offline”) via a visually hidden suffix or aria-describedby.
  • Group. A set is role="group" with aria-label; roving tabindex inside it.
  • Focus visible. The 2px inner stroke on a solid fill, on both themes.
  • Contrast, light. Information label 6.0:1 on its fill; Live label 11.3:1; Maintenance 9.2:1; Offline 13.5:1. Active / selected fills: Information 4.5:1, Live 13.7:1, Maintenance 10.6:1, Offline 18.1:1. All pass. Focus fills: Information #0D598F on #41A8F2 fails (see Known gaps); Live and Maintenance pass with the white label.

Known gaps

  • Information focus: label #0D598F on focus fill #41A8F2 measures 2.85:1 (fail). The 2px inner stroke carries the focus; the label is still readable but under AA. Omitted from Specs as a pass.
  • Information active #FFFFFF on #0B7ACA is 4.52:1 — passes, with no margin at all.
  • Dot stroke is #FFFFFF in dark mode too, so on the dark Offline fill the dot ring is the brightest thing in the chip. Looks intentional in the file; flagging it.
  • No disabled state in Figma. The house rule (remove, don’t disable) follows from that.
  • Small tokens are not published separately (only chip/large/*); Small padding and gap here are measured from the frames.
