What the statuses mean

StatusMeaningWhat to do
SolidThe page answers every question a builder has. Where Figma has not caught up yet, the page says so under “Figma housekeeping”, and the page is the spec until it does.Build against the page.
Has gapsIt works, but something is missing, duplicated or misnamed. The gap is named in the row.Build against it, and read the gap before relying on the missing part.
BrokenFigma itself reports the set as structurally invalid.Do not build against it. Ask Verifi Design.
MissingNo usable component. Tokens or a stub may exist.Use the written interim rule, and expect it to change.

How this list is counted

One row for each component on the finished pages of Trinity Design System John V. Parts that only exist inside another component, such as Slider thumb, Card content, Table cell and Calendar day, are covered on their parent’s page. Charts and Icons are libraries you pick from, so they are not counted here.
