Segmented control

Two to five views of the same data, switched in place. Every segment is the Alternate Button in its Secondary family, the neutral button built to be selected.

Has gaps2 to 5 segments4 sizesSecondary family onlyLight and dark readFigma 63396:1022 ↗Secondary light 63405:4992 ↗Secondary dark 63405:5010 ↗
What changed in this version (October 2026). Rebuilt against Trinity Design System John V. The segments are no longer a mix of Primary and Ghost Buttons: the file now has an Alternate Button (65181:6374) with a real selected state, in primary and hub families, and the segmented control is built from it. The Hub uses the Secondary family only (63405:4992 light, 63405:5010 dark): neutral border, pale-blue selected. The Primary and Accent groups — dark blue in light, brand yellow in dark — are dropped from this page; a view switch is not a call to action. Hover, focus and disabled are their own tokens, light and dark. Small (24) joins the sizes.

Anatomy

  • SegmentsTwo to five Alternate Button instances, joined edge to edge with a −1px gap so the 1px inner borders do not double. Outer corners take the pill (radius 100); inner corners are square (segment-control/inner-radius = 0).
  • Alternate ButtonThe button built for selection, used here in its Secondary family (Figma Type = Secondary, 63405:4992 light / 63405:5010 dark); states default, hover, selected, focus, disabled, loading; sizes xlarge 52, large 40, medium 32, small.
  • Selected segmentThe Alternate Button’s selected state: fill #41A8F2 at 50%, label #171614.
  • Unselected segmentIts default: fill #FFFFFF, 1px #D0CEC8 border, label #171614.
  • LabelBody/sm Medium 14, 1px text padding, 4px gap to an optional 16px icon on either side (prefixIcon / suffixIcon booleans, inherited from Button).
  • TypeFigma’s group-level Type has Primary, Accent, Secondary and Ghost. The Hub uses Secondary only: the Primary and Accent groups put a dark blue (light) and the brand yellow (dark) on a view switch, which reads as a call to action and goes against the system. Only Secondary is on this page.
Why a separate button, and why Secondary. The ordinary Button has no selected state, so a segment could never read as selected; the Alternate Button adds one. And the Secondary family is the one that belongs on a view switch: blue and yellow are the system’s action colours, and a segmented control is not an action.

Behaviour

These are rules, not suggestions. Figma draws the group at every count, type and size, and the Alternate Button at every state. The rules below are the house rules. Build to this.
  • Segmented control or tabs? A segmented control switches between representations of one dataset: All tickets, Fleet map, Phases. Tabs switch between views of one object. If the page title stays and the content changes shape, this is the one.
  • Exactly one selected. No empty state and no deselect. On load, the first segment unless the URL says otherwise.
  • Instant and lossless. If moving away would discard input, this is the wrong component.
  • In the URL. The selected segment is linkable and survives refresh, the same rule as Tabs.
  • Segments size to their labels; they do not stretch to equal widths. One or two words.
  • Placement. Toolbar row, right-aligned, beside the search field. One per toolbar.
  • Icons. Prefix or suffix icon per segment, inherited from Button. Icon-only segments need an accessible name and a tooltip, and only where the glyphs are already understood (list, grid, map).
  • Keyboard. Tab moves between segments; Enter and Space select. Arrow keys are optional.

Guidelines

Do

  • Use it for two to five views of the same data.
  • Order the segments by how often they are used.
  • Keep one selected at all times.
  • Use Large in a toolbar, Medium inside a card.
  • Use the Alternate Button, Secondary family; never a plain Button with a colour override.

Do not

  • Use it for actions. A row of Primary buttons is a button group, not this.
  • Use it as a form input. That is a radio group.
  • Put more than five segments in.
  • Use the Primary, Accent or Ghost types. They are in the file, not in the product: a blue or yellow segment reads as an action.
  • Mix sizes in one toolbar.

Accessibility

  • Group, not radio. role="group" with a name; each segment a <button aria-pressed>. This is a view switch, not a form value.
  • Selection cues. The selected fill is pale blue against white neighbours (1.6:1 light, 2.3:1 dark): under the 3:1 boundary on its own, so the pressed state must be announced and the label must stay the stronger cue. Listed under Known gaps.
  • Focus visible. The 2px outer ink stroke plus the fill change, on both themes.
  • Contrast. Labels: 18:1 unselected, 11.3:1 selected, 15.6:1 focus in light; 13.4:1, 9.1:1 and 15:1 in dark. All pass.
  • Icon-only segments need an accessible name and a tooltip each.

Known gaps

  • Selected fill is weak against its neighbours. #41A8F2 at 50% over white measures 1.6:1 against an unselected segment (dark: #C5E4FB on #2F2D28 is fine at 11:1 but the label drops to #393632). The label and aria-pressed carry selection; a stronger selected fill in the Secondary family would be safer than borrowing Primary’s blue.
  • Dark focus is not drawn for the Alternate Button; this page derives it from hover.
  • The group needs the −1px overlap to hide doubled borders, as the Figma annotation says; the CSS here does the same. Check it in the build.
  • Figma housekeeping. The Primary and Accent groups are still in the set (63405:4523, 63405:4524); the Hub does not use them. The older Segmented Controls and Switch Item sets (56873:24205, 37702:166) are still in the file.
