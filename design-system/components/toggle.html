Toggle

An instant on or off. Flipping it changes the system there and then — there is nothing to press afterwards.

Behaviour

If there is a Save button on the screen, this is the wrong component. A toggle commits on flip. Anything that waits for a form submission is a checkbox.
  • The label says what is on, not what the toggle does. “Live fleet updates”, not “Turn on live updates”.
  • No separate on/off text beside it. The position is the state.
  • If the change can fail — a network call — show the failure and return the toggle to where it was. Never leave it lying.

Guidelines

Do

  • Use it for preferences and live filters that redraw immediately.
  • Keep the label positive and static; it does not change when the state does.
  • Group related toggles under a heading.

Do not

  • Do not use a toggle inside a form with a Save button.
  • Do not use one for a destructive action. That needs a confirm step.
  • Do not add “On” and “Off” text either side — it doubles the reading and halves the clarity.

Specs

  • Track40 × 22
  • Knob18 × 18
  • Inner padding2px
  • Travel18px
  • Gap to label10px
  • Transition0.14s cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
  • Hit target44px minimum

Accessibility

  • Use <input type="checkbox" role="switch">. A screen reader then says “switch, on” rather than “checkbox, checked”.
  • The visible label is the accessible name.
  • Space activates. The change must be announced — if flipping it changes content elsewhere on the page, that region needs aria-live.
  • Do not rely on the track colour alone; the knob position carries the state for anyone who cannot distinguish the fill.

Known gaps

  • The toggle has no hover state. It is the only atom in the library missing one. Until it is drawn, we darken the track slightly, which is a guess.
  • Mixed casing in the variants — disabled: False/True beside value: true/false.
  • A small size exists as a variant but has no published dimensions.
