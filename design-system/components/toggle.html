Toggle

A switch for a setting that takes effect now and can be flipped back. Two sizes, two values, a label that names the setting.

What changed in this version (October 2026). Rebuilt against Trinity Design System John V. The track is now 40 × 24 with a 16px switch and 4px padding (was 40 × 22 / 18 / 2), and there is a published Small size (24 × 16, 8px switch). Off is a real token (#666054) rather than a border colour; disabled is #B6B1A5 at full opacity, not 42% alpha. Focus is a white stroke on the switch plus an ink ring. The Toggle Item (switch + label, gap 4) is the unit, with its own hover state. Page restructured to the Tooltip rulebook.
What it is. The control for “is this on?”. If the answer changes anything the moment you flip it, this is the component.

Try it

Click or press Space. It takes effect immediately — there is no Save. Tab to it and the focus ring appears: a 2px white stroke on the switch and a 1px ink ring outside the track.

Dark

The knob travels 16px in 140ms on the system ease; the track colour crosses in the same time. Under prefers-reduced-motion it jumps.

Anatomy

  • TrackLarge 40 × 24, Small 24 × 16, radius 100. Owns the on / off colour.
  • SwitchLarge 16 × 16 inside 4px padding; Small 8 × 8 inside 4px. Always #FFFFFF. Travels the track width minus itself and the padding: 16px large, 8px small.
  • Element boxSmall sits in a 24px-tall element (top-bottom-margin 4) so both sizes share the same hit height.
  • Label (Toggle Item)Body/sm 14, #171614, 4px from the track. Part of the hit area. Never a sentence; the state of the switch is the answer, so the label names the setting.
  • Focus2px #FFFFFF stroke on the switch (1px small) and a #171614 ring 1px outside the track.
No label inside the track. No ON / OFF text, no tick or cross on the switch. The position is the state; the label beside it says what the state means.

Hover and focus both show the ink ring, 1px off the track (61014:46755 draws hover on the item; the input frame draws focus). Focus adds the white stroke on the switch, which only shows on the dark theme where the switch is dark. Dark is derived: the off track lightens so it still sits against black.

Behaviour

These are rules, not suggestions. Figma draws both sizes and every state. The rules below are the house rules. Build to this.
  • Immediate. Flipping it does the thing. If a change needs a Save button, it is a checkbox in a form, not a toggle.
  • Reversible. A toggle must be safe to flip back. Anything destructive or irreversible gets a button and a confirmation.
  • The label names the setting, not the state. “Live fleet updates”, never “Turn on live updates” or “Enabled”. The switch position is the state.
  • Disabled says why. A disabled toggle keeps its position and gets a reason next to it (“requires a sensor”) or a tooltip; never a bare grey switch.
  • Failure. If the change does not take, the switch snaps back and a Toast says what happened. Do not leave it in the new position with an error elsewhere.
  • Motion. 140ms on the knob and the track colour, system ease. No bounce. Reduced motion: instant.
  • Keyboard. Tab reaches it; Space (and Enter) flips it. Arrow keys do nothing — this is a switch, not a slider.
  • One per row. A list of toggles is a list of rows, label left, switch right-aligned, one setting per row. Never a grid of switches.

Guidelines

Do

  • Use it for settings that apply instantly.
  • Keep the label short and a noun phrase.
  • Put the reason next to a disabled toggle.
  • Use Small in tables and card headers, Large everywhere else.
  • Default a new setting to Off unless the person asked for it.

Do not

  • Use a toggle inside a form that has a Submit.
  • Write ON / OFF in or beside the track.
  • Use it to start a job (“Run report”); that is a button.
  • Mix sizes in one list.
  • Change the switch colour per setting.

  • Large switch16 × 16 · travel 16 · focus stroke 2
  • Small track24 × 16 in a 24px element · radius 100
  • Small switch8 × 8 · travel 8 · focus stroke 1

  • Track off#666054 · switch #FFFFFF — 6.2:1; track on #F0EEEA 5.4:1

Accessibility

  • Role. <input type="checkbox" role="switch">, so it announces as “switch, on / off” and keeps native keyboard and form behaviour.
  • Name. The label text, via the wrapping <label>. The Toggle Item is the component precisely so the name is never missing.
  • Not colour alone. On and off differ by position as well as colour; the switch moves 16px. That is the 1.4.1 cue.
  • Contrast. Track on 15.6:1 and off 5.4:1 against the page; switch 18.1:1 and 6.2:1 against its track. The 3:1 boundary rule (1.4.11) is met by the track against the page in both states.
  • Focus. Two cues: the white stroke on the switch and the ink ring outside the track. Visible on both themes.
  • Hit area. The whole Toggle Item, 24px tall minimum; rows in a settings list pad it to 44px.

Known gaps

  • Light only in Figma. Both frames sit on #F0EEEA; the dark values here are derived and flagged in Specs.
  • Dark off track as derived (#9B958A on #000000) measures 7.1:1 against the page but the white switch on it is 2.97:1 — a hair under the 3:1 boundary. The position cue still carries the state; worth a real dark token.
  • Disabled label #B6B1A5 on #F0EEEA measures 1.9:1. Exempt as disabled; listed, not in Specs.
  • Hover is drawn on the Toggle Item only, not on the bare input. This page applies it to both.
  • Small focus uses a 1px switch stroke; at 8px that is faint. The outer ring does the work.
