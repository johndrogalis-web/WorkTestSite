Verifi Design System/Foundations/Accessibility

Accessibility.

Measured in your browser rather than claimed. The target is WCAG 2.2 level AA, and where something clears AAA as well it is worth knowing — but AA is the bar everything has to reach.

What we are aiming at

AA is the requirement. AAA is a bonus, never a blocker. That distinction settles arguments quickly, because several of the numbers people quote from memory are AAA. If something fails AA it is a defect; if it only fails AAA it is a nice-to-have and should not be logged as a gap.
ThingAA requiresAAA requiresWhat we do
Normal text4.5:17:1Hold 4.5:1. Most of the ink ramp clears 5:1 or better.
Large text ≥24px, or ≥18.66px bold3:14.5:1Headings clear AA comfortably.
Icons, borders and controls you must see3:1—Measured per component. The progress track and two badge dots currently fail.
Touch targets24 × 24 (2.5.8)44 × 44 (2.5.5)24 is the floor we hold. 44 where it costs nothing.
MotionNothing beyond 2.2.2Honour prefers-reduced-motion (2.3.3)We honour it anyway, but it never blocks a component.
Two things this rules out as defects. A 24px control is not a failure because it is under 44px, and a component without a reduced-motion variant is not failing AA. Both used to be logged as gaps on this site and have been removed.

Contrast, measured

Text has to be bright enough against its background for people to read it, including anyone with weaker eyesight and anyone holding a tablet in direct sun. 4.5 to 1 is the pass mark for normal text; 3 to 1 applies to large text only.

Two failures, both in light mode. The faintest ink token, --subtle, is used for placeholders and small print and measures 2.87:1 on white — below even the large-text bar. The next shade up, --soft, reaches 3.79–3.94:1, which is legal at 18px and above but not at the 12–13px it is actually used at. Tracked as open item 03.
Until it is fixed: do not ship placeholder text in --subtle. Use --soft at 14px or larger for anything a user has to read.

Beyond contrast

RuleWhat it means here
Never colour aloneEvery phase label carries its name. Every alert badge carries a count. Colour is the fast read, never the only read.
44px targetsAnything clickable in a toolbar is 44px tall. The person using it may be wearing gloves in a truck yard.
Focus is always visibleA 2px outline at 2px offset, never removed. Tab through any component on this site.
Reduced motionEverything that animates has a path for people who ask for less.
Errors are not just redA red border and a written message. The message is what tells you what to fix.
Real markupReal buttons, real inputs, real tables. A div with a click handler announces nothing.
Before you quote these numbers. This is the standard WCAG 2.1 calculation — the right tool for checking a palette and the wrong one for settling a single borderline case. Anything between 3:1 and 5:1 deserves a look on real hardware in real light.
