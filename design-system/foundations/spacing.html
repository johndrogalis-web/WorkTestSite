Spacing and layout.

Everything sits on a 4px grid. Page gutters are 24px. The Hub content area is a floating card on a slightly darker page, and that one idea drives the whole frame.

The 4px grid

Every dimension in the product is a multiple of four. Not because four is magic, but because one rule that is always true beats a hundred judgement calls.

StepUsed for
4pxIcon to its label, tight internal gaps
8pxBetween related controls, chip padding
10pxToolbar gap, between form options
12pxCard internal padding at small sizes
16pxCard and widget padding
24pxPage gutters, section spacing
32pxBetween major sections
48pxBetween page regions

The app frame

  • Sidebar280px, on a surface slightly darker than the content
  • ContentA floating card — 12px radius, soft shadow, 8px inset from the viewport edge
  • Gutters24px
  • Sidebar items14px text, 10px / 8px padding, 8px radius
  • Sidebar icons16px at 60% opacity, 100% when active
  • Sidebar footerAccount picker in a bordered card, then Profile, Support, Settings
Session scope lives in the sidebar. A separate Account / Division / Region / Plant scope bar appeared in early consultant screens and was cut. It will not be in the Verifi build. The account picker in the sidebar footer is where scope is set.

Page order

Every Hub page stacks in the same order. Sticking to it is most of what makes the product feel like one product rather than twelve.

title row
↓ (stat band)
↓ toolbar
↓ (tabs)
↓ content
↓ (pagination)

  • Page title24px / 500, letter-spacing −0.72px
  • Subtitle12–13px in --soft
  • Right slotEither “Last updated: Today, 9:42 AM” or one page action as an outline pill. Not both.
  • Stat bandFull-width flex row, cells divided by 1px hairlines — not boxed cards. Label 16px above value 40px / 500. 3 to 6 stats.
  • Toolbar44px controls, 10px gap

The toolbar

Left to right, always in this order:

  • Pill search input, with a placeholder listing what is searchable — “Ticket, truck, order, mix, etc.”
  • Filters button with a funnel icon. Active state is a blue border, 6% blue background and a count badge.
  • One quiet primary action — “+ New ticket”, as an outline pill, not filled.
  • Columns menu (eye icon plus chevron).
  • Export, icon-only, 44 × 44.
  • Right-aligned: the segmented view switcher.

Table framing

Full-bleed

  • Full-bleed for a primary page table. No outer border, no clipping. The page gutters are the table edges.

Boxed

  • Boxed for a table inside a widget or card: 1px border, 8px radius, clipped.

The choice is contextual, not aesthetic. See Table.
