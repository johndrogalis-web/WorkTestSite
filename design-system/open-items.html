Open items.

Four questions this system cannot answer on its own, written down rather than guessed at. Everything else on this site is a rule; this page is the list of things that are not yet rules.

Why a page like this exists. A design system that only publishes its settled parts teaches people to trust it uniformly, which means they trust the unsettled parts too. Naming the gaps costs a page and saves the argument that happens six weeks into a sprint.

The register

Numbers are kept as they were so that references on other pages still point at the right item. The gaps in the sequence are the items settled below.

Settled, so nobody reopens them

  • 02 · Loaded phase pill: darkened one shade to #7A7211, 4.95:1. Figma to update the token.
  • 04 · Dark mode for form fields: where Figma has not drawn it, the dark value is the same token in dark mode, and the page showing it is the spec until it is drawn. Applies to every component, not only form fields.
  • 06 · Modal has one size: by decision. Extra small is the modal; anything bigger is a drawer or a page. Header, footer and close rules are on the Modal page.
  • 07 · Truck phase names: the words on screen win — To job, Return to plant. Figma renames the token and the variant.
  • 08 · Table conflicts: ABC Repro, Unicons, 16px selectors, and the selected tint is primary/50 after all. Sort and empty state are also decided on the Table page. Figma to rebind.
  • 09 · Icons: Unicons only for new work, Feather frozen, no Font Awesome, never mixed in one view. The interim rule is the rule.
  • 10 · Badge dot: a 1px inside ring on badge/dot/stroke, in Figma and on the site. Both dot failures gone.
  • 11 · Progress and slider tracks: one track token, one 1px ring in #7F796C, both components. Figma to align the slider.
  • 12 · Tag colour pairs: the default pair is the approved pair; any other pair is measured in both themes before use and proposed back as a token. The same rule as Badge.
  • 13 · Slider focus: a 3px ring in the selection colour, keyboard only; disabled at 42%; arrow, page, Home and End keys defined. Figma to draw.
  • 14 · Tooltip behaviour: delay, dismissal, trigger and touch rules are all on the Tooltip page, which is Solid.
  • Nav and segmented active colour: blue in light, lime in dark. The lime-in-light screenshot was an error, not intent.
  • Table framing: full-bleed for primary page tables, boxed for tables inside widgets and cards.
  • Stat band order: label above the number.
  • Scope bar: cut. Session scope lives in the sidebar account picker and the scope bar will not be in the Verifi build.
  • Phase pills flip with the theme. The static solid-in-both-modes prototype is overruled.
  • Yellow is a domain boundary, not a conflict. Marketing call to action; product dark-mode selection. Both correct.

Two errors inside the brand file itself

  • Verifi Yellow has two values. #E3F200 on the primary palette page, #F3F85B on the supporting palette page. The primary value is the one in use.
  • One grey has two names. The same warm dark grey is called Grey 650 on the palette page and Grey 700 on the contrast page.

Raising something

The system is a starting point and it will change. Two rules hold while it does: changes are agreed with Verifi Design before they ship, and every colour pair that lands passes AA. If you find another open question, it belongs here rather than in a thread. Brand questions go to Brittany Cool; product questions go to Verifi Design. Include the node ID — every component page lists them.
