Components · Navigators

Getting around.

Five components that answer “where am I” and “how do I get back”. None of them change anything; that is what makes them navigation.

Tabs or segmented control?

These two look alike and mean different things, and the distinction is worth holding onto.

Tabs — wayfinding

  • Peer views of the same object: Overview, Sensors, History for one truck.
  • Content swaps in place. Switching is instant and loses nothing.
  • Two to eight of them.

Segmented — parameters

  • Different representations of one dataset: All tickets, Fleet map, Phases.
  • You are reconfiguring the current view, not moving to another one.
  • Two to four of them. On the choosing page.
Neither is a stepper. If the views are steps in a sequence, or switching away loses unsaved input, you need a stepper — and no stepper component exists. Talk to Verifi Design rather than dressing tabs up as one.

The Hub sidebar

The Hub’s primary navigation is a 280px rail on a surface slightly darker than the content, and the content sits on it as a floating card at 12px radius, inset 8px from the viewport edge.

  • Width280px
  • TopThe logotype.
  • Items14px text, 10px / 8px padding, 8px radius, 16px icon at 60% opacity — 100% when active
  • GroupsCollapse behind chevrons.
  • New featuresAn outlined blue pill reading “New”.
  • Footer, alwaysAccount picker in a bordered card, then Profile, Support, Settings.
  • Observed itemsHome, Insights, Tickets, Trucks, Mixtures, Return concrete.
Session scope lives in the sidebar. A separate Account / Division / Region / Plant scope bar appeared in early consultant screens and was cut. It will not be in the Verifi build. Do not reintroduce it — the account picker in the sidebar footer is where scope is set.

Page order

Every Hub page stacks in the same order, and sticking to it is most of what makes the product feel like one product.

title row
↓ (stat band)
↓ toolbar
↓ (tabs)
↓ content
↓ (pagination)

  • Page title24px / 500, letter-spacing −0.72px
  • Subtitle12–13px in --soft
  • Right slotEither “Last updated: Today, 9:42 AM” or one page action as an outline pill. Not both.
  • Gutters24px
  • Toolbar44px controls, 10px gap — search, Filters, one quiet action, Columns, export; view switcher right-aligned

Detail opens in a drawer, not a page

Clicking an entity in the Hub slides a drawer over a scrim rather than navigating away, so the list you were reading stays underneath.

  • Scrimrgba(54,50,45,0.5)
  • Widthclamp(860px, 100% − 280–380px, 1400px)
  • Radius16px, left corners only
  • Motion0.38s cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) slide
  • ChromeTitle left; previous / next arrows and a solid close on the right.
  • Body orderSummary chip row, then key-value rows at 32px, then bordered cards for alerts, each with one quiet outline action.

Known gaps

  • Top navigation is structurally broken. Figma reports its variant properties as invalid. Do not build against it.
  • The entire Hub sidebar cluster exists twice. Instances must reference 56362:*, never 57725:*.
  • Tabs have no defined overflow behaviour past eight. The interim rule: scroll the strip, never wrap to a second row.
  • Breadcrumb links have no hover or focus state drawn. Treat them as links and give them the link treatment.
  • Pagination is a single component with no states, orphaned from the table it serves.
